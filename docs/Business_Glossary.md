# Business Glossary – ERDP

**Sprint 3 deliverable** · Common definitions of the business terms and KPIs used in the warehouse and the Power BI dashboard, so every team reads the numbers the same way.

## 1. Business terms

| Term | Definition | Where in the warehouse |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales channel** | Where a sale happened: **POS** (in-store till) or **E-Commerce** (online order) | dim_channel |
| **Transaction / Invoice** | One POS receipt. Identified by InvoiceNo; one invoice has many lines | fact_sales.transaction_ref (POS) |
| **Order** | One online purchase. Identified by order_id; one order has many lines | fact_sales.transaction_ref (E-Commerce) |
| **Sales line** | One product on one invoice or order. The grain of fact_sales | fact_sales |
| **Line amount** | Quantity × unit price for one sales line. Negative for returns | fact_sales.line_amount |
| **Cancelled invoice** | A POS invoice whose number starts with "C". Kept for audit, excluded from revenue | fact_sales.is_cancelled |
| **Return** | A sales line with negative quantity (goods brought back) | fact_sales.is_return |
| **Valid sale** | A sales line that is not cancelled, not a return, has a price above 0, and is a real product | Filter used by all KPI queries |
| **Customer** | A person or business registered in the CRM. Identified by customer_id | dim_customer |
| **Unknown customer** | Placeholder (key −1) for sales with no customer ID, mainly walk-in or guest purchases | dim_customer, key −1 |
| **Product** | An item in the inventory master. Identified by product_id (stock code) | dim_product |
| **Non-product code** | A code that is not a physical product: POST (postage), DOT (dotcom postage), C2 (carriage), M (manual), D (discount), BANK CHARGES. Excluded from product KPIs | dim_product.is_non_product |
| **Stock on hand** | Units of a product available in inventory at the snapshot date | fact_inventory_snapshot.stock_quantity |
| **Reorder level** | Stock level at which the product must be reordered | dim_product.reorder_level |
| **Below reorder** | Stock on hand is lower than the reorder level | fact_inventory_snapshot.is_below_reorder |
| **Stock value** | Stock on hand × list price | fact_inventory_snapshot.stock_value |
| **Purchase order (PO)** | An order placed by ABC Retail with a supplier | fact_supplier_orders |
| **Order status** | State of a PO: Pending, In Transit, Delivered | fact_supplier_orders.order_status |
| **Expected lead time** | Days between PO order date and expected delivery date | fact_supplier_orders.expected_lead_time_days |
| **Bronze / Silver / Gold** | Raw copy of source data / cleaned data / business-ready star schema | Schemas bronze, silver, gold |
| **Surrogate key** | Number generated by the warehouse (e.g. product_key) used to join facts to dimensions | *_key columns |
| **Business key** | The ID used in the source system (e.g. product_id) | dim_*.*_id columns |

## 2. KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Query (`sql/06_reporting_queries.sql`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Daily Sales Revenue** | Value of valid sales per day | SUM(line_amount) of valid sales, grouped by date | #1 |
| **Top Selling Products** | Products with the highest valid sales revenue | SUM(line_amount) per product, top 10 | #2 |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Average value of one invoice or order | Revenue ÷ COUNT(DISTINCT transaction_ref) | #3 |
| **Regional Sales Performance** | Revenue, orders and customers per country | SUM(line_amount) grouped by country | #4 |
| **Return & Refund Analysis** | Share of sales lines returned, and refunded value | Return lines ÷ all lines; −SUM(line_amount) of returns | #5 |
| **Customer Retention Rate** | Share of known customers who bought on more than one day | Customers with > 1 purchase day ÷ known customers | #6 |
| **Stock Availability** | Share of products at or above their reorder level | Products not below reorder ÷ all products | #7 |
| **Reorder Alert** | Products below reorder level and their shortfall | reorder_level − stock_quantity | #8 |
| **Inventory Turnover Ratio** | How many times stock is sold through | Units sold ÷ stock on hand | #9 |
| **Supplier Performance** | Purchase value, lead time and status mix per supplier | SUM(total_cost), AVG(lead time), count by status | #10 |
| **Supplier On-Time Delivery** | Share of POs delivered by the expected date | *Not available: source has no actual delivery date* | — |
| **Warehouse Fulfilment Efficiency** | — | *Not available: no warehouse data in the sources* | — |
